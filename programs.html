From hands-on workshops to student-led school chapters, STEM Scholars gives students many ways to discover what STEM can be. Explore what we offer and find a place to start.

STEM workshop

STEM workshops and events

Our workshops turn curiosity into something students can do with their hands. Designed for elementary, middle, and high school students, each session pairs a hands-on activity with a look at the real careers behind it.

Students might write their first lines of code, take on an engineering challenge, run a science experiment, explore robotics, or learn what different STEM careers look like. Activities are built so beginners can jump right in.

STEM Scholars chapters

Chapters put our mission inside schools. A chapter is a student-led group that brings STEM activities, resources, events, and career exploration to its own campus: run by students, for students.

Our current chapters are at Folsom High School and Sutter Middle School, and we'd love to help more schools start their own.
