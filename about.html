STEM Scholars FoundationAbout

We help students see the many paths STEM can take them down. Learn who we are, what we believe, and how STEM Scholars Foundation got started.

Our mission

STEM Scholars Foundation empowers K–12 students to explore STEM through career education, hands-on learning, mentorship, and accessible resources. Our mission is to help students discover the possibilities within STEM, develop their interests and skills, and connect with opportunities that support their educational and future career goals.

Who we are

STEM Scholars Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping K–12 students explore and engage with science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). We provide students with opportunities to learn about STEM fields and careers through educational workshops, mentorship, hands-on projects, career exploration, resources, and volunteer opportunities.

Through our programs and school chapters, we aim to make STEM education and career exploration more accessible, engaging, and relevant to students at different stages of their education. We help students discover their interests, build skills, explore potential career paths, and connect with resources and opportunities that can support their future goals.

As we grow, we plan to keep expanding our programs, chapters, partnerships, and educational resources to reach more students and introduce them to the wide range of possibilities within STEM.

Our story

It started with a question: where do we even begin?

As high school juniors, we were both drawn to STEM, but we had a hard time figuring out where to start and what to do next. The more we looked into the paths we were pursuing, the more we realized we didn't fully understand them: what people in these careers actually do, what to study, and how to get real experience.

We couldn't imagine we were the only students asking those questions. So we set out to build what we wished we'd had: a place where students can explore STEM careers, try hands-on projects, and connect with peers, mentors, and opportunities before the big decisions arrive.

That idea became STEM Scholars Foundation. Today it lives in our school chapters and workshops, and we're growing it one school at a time.

What we believe

Accessibility

STEM opportunities should be accessible to every student.

Exploration

Students should have opportunities to discover different STEM paths.

Hands-on learning

STEM is best understood by actually experiencing it.

Community

Students benefit from learning alongside peers, mentors, educators, and professionals.
